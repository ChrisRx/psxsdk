    Examples

    The examples are ports of Sony's Net Yaroze samples and demos, and of one of the games members wrote, chosen to put a different part of the machine to work each:

    Example Original What it exercises
    check Sony SDK sample sprites, controller, CD reads, sequenced music
    chrome S. Ashley, 1997 GTE, 3D library, lighting, environment mapping
    clone S. Ashley, 1997 first-person maze shooter: fog, subdivision, SPU voices
    dungeon Mizuki, 1996 first-person maze built per frame, fog, two lights
    offscr L. Evans, 1997 drawing into a texture at run time
    tunnel L. Evans, 1997 endless tunnel shooter, three lights, VAB sound effects
    flame S. Ashley, 1997 a sheet of fire: a TMD built in memory, recolored every frame, at 640 by 480
    blow S. Ashley, 1997 a model viewer that blows its model apart, with every light and fog parameter on a menu
    dino Sony, 1996 a walking dinosaur: GTE vertex interpolation, transfer functions, a full-screen background, reverb
    invaders L. Evans, 1996 Space Invaders in sprites, with a high score table
    breakout L. Evans, 1996 Breakout in sprites, with the ball from a texture table
    dogtale Ira Rainey, 1997 A Dog Tale, a platform game: parallax scrolling, a screen grab spun away, PAL
    car Sony, 1995 a textured car driving in from the distance, at sixty frames a second or thirty
    oden Sony, 1993 the lighting calculation: three shapes under three lights you aim and color
    morph S. Ashley, 1997 a textured sheet morphing into a cylinder, a torus and a sphere, vertex by vertex
    mdec new full-color video through the motion decoder, driven by its registers

    Every example runs in the browser, in the emulator this repository contains, on the pages that follow and together in the player. Each example's data/ directory holds the files it reads off the CD, each registered in its main.go with the disc package so that mkpsxiso places it, and task disc NAME=check builds one. The header comment of each main.go notes where the port departs from the original and why.
