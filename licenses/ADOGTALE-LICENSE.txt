The dogtale example is a port to Go of A Dog Tale, by Ira Rainey, written
with his source as the guide. Its pictures are his: examples/dogtale/assets/DOG.DAT
is the first part of the dog.dat in his repository, the twenty-four pictures
the game draws with: https://github.com/irarainey/a-dog-tale

His code and graphics are under the MIT license, which follows. The rest of
his dog.dat is the tune and the sound bank, which are not his: they are the
sample sound from Sony's Net Yaroze SDK, as his message in the game says,
the one the check example plays, and the port reads them from the SDK's own
files, SAMPLE1.SEQ, STD0.VH and STD0.VB, beside DOG.DAT.
